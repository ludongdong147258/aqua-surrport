Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my Pro subscription?

If you've reinstalled the app or switched devices, go to the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchase. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your device, open Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions (or your App Store account settings), select AquaFishTank, and choose cancel or change plan.

How do I back up my data?

All your data lives on your device. Go to Profile → Backup & Restore to export a backup file. Keep the file somewhere safe (e.g. iCloud Drive). You can import it later on any device to restore your tanks and records.

I deleted the app — is my data gone?

Since AquaFishTank stores everything locally and requires no account, uninstalling the app removes your data. Restore from a backup file if you exported one previously.
