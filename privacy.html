Privacy Policy

AquaFishTank

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Overview

AquaFishTank is designed to be private by default. The app works fully offline, requires no account, and does not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data.

  • No sign-up or login — we never ask for your name, email, or phone number.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No ads or ad networks.
  • All app data stays on your device.

Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device only:

  • Your tanks, livestock, and fish favorites
  • Records: water changes, water parameters, feeding, maintenance, expenses, breeding, cycling, and growth
  • App settings and preferences

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export a backup file yourself. Backups are written to a location you choose and are not uploaded anywhere by us. Deleting the app removes all local data.

Subscriptions (Pro)

AquaFishTank offers an optional Pro subscription. Payments are processed entirely by Apple; we never see or store your payment details.

To verify your subscription status, the app uses RevenueCat, our subscription-management service. RevenueCat processes the transaction identifiers provided by Apple in order to validate your entitlement. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy for details.

Terms of Use

Your use of the app is governed by Apple's standard End User License Agreement.

Children's Privacy

The app does not collect any personal information and is safe for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any data from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy
